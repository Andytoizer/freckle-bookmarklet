Send to Freckle is installed

  1. Pin it

    Click the puzzle piece next to the address bar, then the pin beside Send to Freckle.

  2. Open the side panel and sign in

    Clicking the icon opens it. It stays open while you move between pages.

  3. Send pages

    Pick a workflow once per kind of page. After that it's one click, or Alt+Shift+F from any tab.
